ABOUT THE ARTIST

Curiosity turned into sound.

“I've always been interested in the intersection of music and technology. I love sharing this part of my life with others. Thank you for sharing my music with the world.”

Dr Sorgenstein is an independent creative project shaped by a lifelong interest in communication, technology, leadership, education, and experimentation. Christopher Sorge brings an unusually interdisciplinary background to the project: graduate study in information technology management, public administration, software engineering training, project and business analysis experience, military leadership, and classroom instruction.

That mix informs a creative philosophy centered on exploration rather than a single genre. The goal is simple: make memorable music, keep experimenting, and use technology as a tool for deeper creative connection.
