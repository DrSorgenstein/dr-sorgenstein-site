Case file No. 001

A late-arriving obsession

I've always had a love of music. In my earlier years I dabbled in electronic music and writing music, but I never pursued it seriously — until now. Why not?

I feel as though I've lived a thousand lives, with careers spanning finance, information technology, and, more recently, education. Music is the one that finally stuck around long enough to get a name of its own.

I hope my music brings you as much joy as it brings me. Thank you for listening.

— C. Sorge, aka Dr. Sorgenstein
