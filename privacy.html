Dr Sorgenstein Website Privacy

The mailing-list form collects the email address you voluntarily submit, your consent to receive promotional messages, the signup source, and the signup timestamp. The default package stores this information in a private Cloudflare KV namespace controlled by the site operator.

The AI virtual host sends the text you enter to the configured serverless backend and OpenAI for the purpose of generating a response. Do not submit sensitive personal information through the chat.

Mailing-list data should be used only for Dr Sorgenstein release announcements and related promotional updates. Subscribers should be given a practical method to unsubscribe or request deletion.

This starter privacy notice should be reviewed and customized before a large-scale public launch, especially if analytics, advertising tools, cookies, or a third-party email marketing service are added.
